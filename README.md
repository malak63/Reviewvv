Here are clear, personal-sounding answers you can use for Part C: Reflection, based on your election project involving HIV & TB and free campus Wi-Fi:

1. What did you learn about HIV and TB, and the stigma associated with these diseases, during your election project?
    I learned that HIV and TB can affect anyone and that stigma can prevent people from getting tested or seeking treatment. I also learned that education and support can help reduce discrimination.
2. What did you learn about the challenges around providing free Wi-Fi on campus?
    I learned that providing free Wi-Fi requires good funding, reliable infrastructure and enough network capacity. I also learned that many students depend on Wi-Fi for their studies, so it needs to be accessible and reliable.
3. What did you learn about your problem-solving skills?
    I learned that I can solve problems by thinking of different solutions and choosing the most practical one. I also learned not to give up when something does not work the first time.
4. How did your team working skills develop during this project?
    My teamwork skills improved because I learned to communicate better, listen to other people’s ideas and share responsibilities. We also learned how to work together towards the same goal.
5. Evaluate your group’s election campaign including yourself. What could you have done differently? What worked well? What did not work well?
    Our campaign worked well because we had clear ideas and worked together to present our message. I could have participated more actively in some parts of the campaign and managed my time better. One thing that did not work well was that we sometimes had difficulty coordinating everyone’s ideas and schedules.
6. Why do you think it is important to vote in elections?
    I think voting is important because it gives people a voice in choosing the leaders and policies that affect their lives. It also allows citizens to take part in making decisions about their future.
7. How do you feel about yourself as a voter?
    I feel more confident and responsible as a voter because this project helped me understand why voting matters. I now feel that I should make informed decisions rather than simply voting without knowing about the issues.
8. Why do you think the role of the IEC in elections is important? Give two reasons.
    The IEC is important because it helps organise elections fairly and makes sure that people can vote. It also helps protect the integrity of elections by managing the voting and counting process.
9. How will you apply good voter behavior before, during and after elections?

* Before elections: I will learn about the different parties and their policies so that I can make an informed decision.
* During elections: I will vote peacefully, follow the rules and respect other voters’ choices.
* After elections: I will respect the results and continue to be an active and responsible citizen.
